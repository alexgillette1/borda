Privacy Policy

How Borda collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last Updated: October 2026 Effective Date: October 9, 2026

Borda ("we," "us," or "our") operates a gamified sweepstakes savings platform that allows users to deposit money into savings accounts, earn spins on a slot machine, and win fractional stock shares and Bitcoin. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Borda mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you create an account, join our waitlist, or use our Service, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Identity Information: Full legal name, date of birth, Social Security Number (SSN) or Tax Identification Number (TIN), and government-issued identification documents as required for brokerage and banking compliance.
  • Contact Information: Email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Waitlist Information: Email address and referral source submitted through our waitlist signup forms.
  • Account Credentials: Username, password (stored in hashed form), and authentication tokens.

Financial Information

To facilitate savings deposits, sweepstakes entries, stock share awards, and Bitcoin transactions, we collect:

  • Bank Account Details: Bank name, routing number, and account number for linking your savings account with our partner banks.
  • Brokerage Information: Account identifiers, portfolio data, and transaction histories associated with your Alpaca Securities brokerage account.
  • Bitcoin Wallet Information: Wallet addresses and transaction data processed through Lightspark for Bitcoin-related features.
  • Transaction Records: Deposit amounts, withdrawal requests, sweepstakes spin results, stock share awards, and dividend records.
  • Tax Information: Information necessary to generate IRS Forms 1099 and other tax documents for reportable winnings and investment income.

Device and Usage Data

We automatically collect certain technical information when you access the Service:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, and hardware model.
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, time and date of visits, time spent on pages, and clickstream data.
  • Usage Analytics: Feature engagement metrics, spin frequency, savings deposit patterns, app session duration, and navigation paths.
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location derived from your IP address (used for regulatory compliance and to verify you are in an eligible jurisdiction).

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activity, remember your preferences, and analyze the performance of our Service. For more detail, see Section 10: Cookies & Tracking Technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Account Management and Service Delivery

  • Create, maintain, and secure your Borda account.
  • Process savings deposits and withdrawals through our partner banks.
  • Execute stock share awards through Alpaca Securities.
  • Process Bitcoin transactions through Lightspark.
  • Verify your identity in compliance with Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations.
  • Maintain records of your sweepstakes entries, spin results, and prize history.

Sweepstakes Operations

  • Administer the slot machine sweepstakes, including determining spin eligibility, generating random outcomes, and distributing fractional stock share prizes.
  • Ensure sweepstakes integrity and compliance with applicable state and federal sweepstakes laws.
  • Generate required regulatory filings and tax documentation for sweepstakes winnings.

Communications

  • Send transactional emails and push notifications regarding your account activity, deposits, withdrawals, and prize awards.
  • Deliver waitlist updates and early-access invitations via email.
  • Send promotional communications about new features, offers, or events (with your consent, where required).
  • Respond to your customer support inquiries and feedback.

Analytics and Product Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the Service, develop new features, and optimize user experience.
  • Conduct A/B testing and measure the effectiveness of product changes.
  • Generate aggregated, de-identified analytics reports for internal business purposes.

Fraud Prevention and Security

  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraudulent transactions, unauthorized access, and other illegal or suspicious activities.
  • Monitor for abuse of the sweepstakes system, including multi-accounting, bot activity, and manipulation.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and protect the rights, property, and safety of Borda, our users, and the public.

Legal and Regulatory Compliance

  • Comply with applicable federal and state financial regulations, including SEC, FINRA, FinCEN, and state banking requirements.
  • Fulfill reporting obligations to the IRS and other tax authorities.
  • Respond to subpoenas, court orders, and lawful government requests.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with the following categories of recipients:

Service Providers and Partners

  • Alpaca Securities LLC: We share identity, financial, and tax information with Alpaca Securities, our registered broker-dealer partner, to open and maintain your brokerage account, execute fractional stock share awards, and comply with securities regulations. Alpaca Securities is regulated by FINRA and the SEC and maintains its own privacy policy governing the data it receives.
  • Lightspark: We share wallet address and transaction data with Lightspark to facilitate Bitcoin transactions, including the receipt of Bitcoin prizes and any related transfers. Lightspark processes this data in accordance with its own privacy and security practices.
  • Partner Banks: We share identity and financial information with our FDIC-insured partner banks to open and maintain your savings accounts, process deposits and withdrawals, and ensure compliance with federal banking regulations.
  • Resend: We share your email address with Resend, our email delivery service provider, to send transactional emails (account confirmations, deposit receipts, prize notifications), waitlist communications, and, where you have opted in, promotional emails. Resend processes your email address solely to deliver messages on our behalf.
  • Identity Verification Providers: We share identity information with third-party KYC/AML verification services to verify your identity and comply with applicable financial regulations.
  • Analytics Providers: We share de-identified usage data with analytics providers to help us understand how the Service is used and improve performance.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Your data is stored on servers maintained by our cloud hosting providers, who process data on our behalf under strict contractual data protection obligations.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service, including investigation of potential violations.
  • Detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues.
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Borda, our users, or the public as required or permitted by law.

Business Transfers

If Borda is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, receivership, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on the Service of any change in ownership or uses of your personal information, as well as any choices you may have regarding your personal information.

With Your Consent

We may share your information with third parties when you have given us explicit consent to do so.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. Beyond that, we retain your data as necessary to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, including:

  • Financial Records: Transaction records, brokerage account data, and tax documentation are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years as required by SEC, FINRA, and IRS regulations.
  • KYC/AML Records: Identity verification records are retained for a minimum of five (5) years following account closure, as required by the Bank Secrecy Act and FinCEN regulations.
  • Sweepstakes Records: Prize award records, spin histories, and odds-related data are retained for a minimum of four (4) years to comply with state sweepstakes regulations and IRS reporting requirements.
  • Waitlist Data: If you sign up for our waitlist but do not create an account, we retain your email address until you unsubscribe or for up to twenty-four (24) months, whichever comes first.
  • Usage and Analytics Data: De-identified usage data may be retained indefinitely for product improvement and analytical purposes.

When personal information is no longer required, we securely delete or de-identify it in accordance with our data retention and disposal procedures.

5. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect your personal and financial information, including:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Sensitive personal and financial data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Controls: Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis. We employ role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for internal systems.
  • Infrastructure Security: Our infrastructure is hosted on SOC 2 Type II certified cloud platforms with 24/7 monitoring, intrusion detection systems, and regular security audits.
  • Secure Financial Integrations: Connections to Alpaca Securities, Lightspark, and our partner banks use dedicated, encrypted API channels with tokenized credentials. We do not store your full bank account credentials on our servers.
  • Incident Response: We maintain a documented incident response plan and will notify affected users and relevant authorities of any data breach in accordance with applicable law.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to continuously improving our security practices.

  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit the processing of your personal data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing or where processing is based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

10. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use the following types of cookies and similar technologies:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function. They enable core features such as authentication, session management, security protections, and fraud prevention. You cannot opt out of essential cookies because the Service will not work without them.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the Service by collecting information about pages visited, features used, session duration, and error occurrences. Analytics data is aggregated and used to improve the Service. We may use third-party analytics tools that set their own cookies.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences and settings (such as language, display, or notification preferences) so that we can provide a more personalized experience.

Marketing and Advertising Cookies

If used, these cookies track your activity across websites to deliver more relevant advertising. We will obtain your consent before placing non-essential marketing cookies. You may opt out at any time through our cookie settings or your browser preferences.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain parts of the Service. For more information about managing cookies, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that a user does not want their online activity tracked. Because there is no universally accepted standard for how to respond to DNT signals, the Service does not currently respond to DNT browser signals. We will update this policy if a uniform standard is established.
