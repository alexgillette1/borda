Multi-Payline System

Borda uses a 3×3 grid slot machine with up to 3 paylines. Each payline is checked independently for a 3-of-a-kind match. Only matching all 3 symbols on a payline counts as a win.

Payline 1 — Middle Row: The center row across all 3 reels (always active).

Payline 2 — Diagonal ↘: Top-left → center → bottom-right (active with 3-line play).

Payline 3 — Diagonal ↗: Bottom-left → center → top-right (active with 3-line play).

Playing 3 lines costs 3× the share multiplier in spins (e.g., 3 lines at 2× = 6 spins per pull). Each winning payline awards shares independently.

How Wins Are Determined

When a spin produces a 3-of-a-kind on a payline, the winning stock is determined in two steps:

Step 1 — Tier selection: A rarity tier is chosen using the tier weights below. Common stocks (under $50) are selected 55% of the time, while legendary stocks ($650+) are selected only 2% of the time.

Step 2 — Stock selection: Within the chosen tier, the specific stock is selected proportionally to its weight (see table below). Stocks with higher weights within a tier appear more frequently.
