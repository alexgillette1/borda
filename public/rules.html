4. How to Enter — Savings Deposit Method

During the Sweepstakes Period, eligible entrants who have a valid Borda account may earn entries ("Spins") by maintaining a savings deposit balance within the Borda application. Entries are earned automatically as follows:

Entry Earning Method

Each eligible entrant earns one (1) Spin for every one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) in average daily savings balance maintained in the entrant's Borda savings account. Spin allocations are calculated and credited daily at the close of each Entry Period based on the entrant's savings balance as of the daily calculation time.

For example, an entrant with a $5,000 daily average savings balance would earn 5 Spins per day. An entrant with a $500 daily average savings balance would earn 0 Spins for that day via this method. Fractional Spins are not awarded; the calculation is rounded down to the nearest whole number.

Depositing money into a Borda savings account constitutes an investment/savings decision and is not a purchase or payment for purposes of entering the Sweepstakes. The Sweepstakes entry is a bonus provided to savers; all savings deposits are held in FDIC-insured accounts at partner banking institutions and may be withdrawn at any time, subject to the terms of the Borda Savings Account Agreement.

A purchase, deposit, or payment will not increase an entrant's chances of winning on any individual Spin. Each Spin carries the same fixed odds of winning regardless of the entrant's deposit amount, account balance, or total number of Spins earned.

6. Spin Mechanics

Each Spin entitles the entrant to one play of the Borda slot machine game. The game operates as follows:

Slot Machine Configuration

The Borda slot machine is a 3-reel, 3-row digital slot machine displaying a 3×3 grid of stock ticker symbols. Each reel contains symbols representing the 25 stocks available in the Sweepstakes prize pool. The outcome of each Spin is determined by a cryptographically secure pseudorandom number generator (CSPRNG) at the time the Spin is executed. All outcomes are final and non-reversible.

Paylines

The slot machine features up to three (3) paylines:

  • Payline 1 — Center Row: The middle row across all three reels (always active on every Spin).
  • Payline 2 — Diagonal (top-left to bottom-right): Active only when the entrant selects 3-line play.
  • Payline 3 — Diagonal (bottom-left to top-right): Active only when the entrant selects 3-line play.

A winning combination occurs when all three (3) positions on a single payline display the same stock ticker symbol (a "3-of-a-kind"). Each payline is evaluated independently. An entrant may win on zero, one, two, or all three paylines in a single Spin.

Share Multiplier

Before each Spin, the entrant may select a share multiplier of 1×, 2×, 3×, or 5×. The multiplier determines both the number of Spins consumed per play and the number of fractional or whole shares awarded per winning payline:

The share multiplier does not affect the odds of winning on any given payline. It affects only the number of Spins consumed and the quantity of shares awarded upon a win.

  1. Tier selection: A rarity tier is selected according to the fixed tier weights: Common (55%), Uncommon (25%), Rare (12%), Epic (6%), Legendary (2%).
